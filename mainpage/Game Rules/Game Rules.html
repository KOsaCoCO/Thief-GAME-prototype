Game Rules

You're a thief facing off against a monster. Your hand of cards is your loot and your health at the same time — every card you hold keeps you in the fight, and every card you take is one the monster can't use against you.

  1. Lose every card and you're out. Strip the monster of everything it has — hand and field alike — and you win.
  2. There are 60 cards, shuffled fresh into three suits each game — Circle, Square, Triangle — so no two runs play out the same. See Card Info for what each one looks like.
  3. Suits have a pecking order: Square muscles past anything, Circle handles Circle and Triangle, and Triangle only manages other Triangles. Same suit on both sides? Higher number wins.
  4. On your turn, click a card and pick your move — Gamble on whether the monster is hiding something higher or lower in the same suit, or Play it: lay it out as bait, or size up a card already on the field and try to take it.
  5. Guess wrong on a gamble and the monster keeps your card. Reach for something you can't beat and you walk away with nothing too.
  6. Leave a card on the field the monster can beat and it'll come for it — but you get one shot to wrestle it back in a quick tug-of-war before it's gone for good.
  7. Call a gamble right and one of your cards earns a small + mark. Win a fight with a marked card and you scoop up a bonus card along with your prize.
  8. If neither side has a move to make, a Bonus Action breaks the stalemate with its own snatch-and-guess round — and whoever comes out ahead is handed a strange marked card, worth playing the moment you get it.
  9. Don't sit on a decision too long. Hesitate, and the monster stops waiting and makes its own move.
